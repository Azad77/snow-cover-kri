# Figures

## Figures in the manuscript

These five are produced by `scripts/make_manuscript_figures.py` and appear in the paper with
the numbers used here.

| File | Manuscript | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `fig11_climatic_drivers.png` | Fig. 11 | Trends in winter temperature, 0 °C isotherm elevation, precipitation and snowfall fraction |
| `fig12_correlation_matrix.png` | Fig. 12 | Detrended correlations between the snow metrics and the local and large-scale drivers |
| `fig13_attribution.png` | Fig. 13 | LMG variance partitioning and decomposition of the observed trends |
| `fig14_snow_cover_duration.png` | Fig. 14 | Snow cover duration from daily MOD10A1, and its temperature sensitivity |
| `fig15_hydrological_response.png` | Fig. 15 | Snowmelt and runoff climatologies, and the seasonal partition of runoff |

Figures 1–10 of the manuscript were produced outside this repository (study-area map,
methodological workflow, and the descriptive snow-cover figures) and are not reproduced here.
